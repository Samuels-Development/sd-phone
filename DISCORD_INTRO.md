# 📱 sd-phone & sd-tablet

**A free, open source phone and tablet ecosystem for FiveM.** One codebase, two devices, everything shared.

Built for **QBox, QBCore and ESX**.

## What you get

- **48 server-backed apps.** Banking, garages, housing, a police MDT, a working newsroom, ride-hailing with real player drivers
- **A social ecosystem players actually log into.** Squawk, Photogram and Vibez, with real accounts, posts, stories and DMs
- **A live game-view camera.** Take real photos in-world, then post them
- **Online multiplayer games**, 11 languages, optional SIM cards
- **Real props in 8 colours.** The item a player carries tints the on-screen frame *and* the model in their hands. Pull out a red phone, everyone sees a red phone
- **A tablet that shares everything.** Same apps, same accounts, same data, bigger screen. Log in on the phone, pick up the tablet, already signed in

## 🔁 Coming from lb-phone?

It's a **drop-in replacement.** Your scripts and custom apps keep working unmodified, and a first-boot migration brings your players across instead of resetting them: numbers, contacts, call history, SMS threads, photos, notes, mail, wallet history, and their social accounts with posts and followers. Even the app logins, so players open the phone already signed in.

## 🛠️ Build your own apps

Third-party apps install at runtime. Templates for plain JS, React, Vue and Svelte. lb-phone apps work here too.
